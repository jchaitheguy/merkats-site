# How it works

Merkats helps a group of people cook together — whether you've known each other for years or just met.

1. **Find your group.** Join a Merkats *den* if you're new to cooking and want to learn alongside others, or join a Merkats *kitchen* run by an experienced cook.
2. **Plan the meals.** See who's in for dinner, set the menu, and build one shared grocery list so nobody buys the same thing twice.
3. **Split the work.** Prep, cooking and cleanup are shared as shifts, so everyone pitches in and no one is stuck with all of it.
4. **Split the cost.** Groceries are split across the people eating, so cooking together costs less than cooking alone.
5. **Stay in touch.** Chat with your group, swap recipes, and sign off on chores right in the app.

<h2 id="host">Seasoned chef?</h2>

Host your own Merkats kitchen, or get paid to lead other Merkats kitchens and teach new cooks along the way.
